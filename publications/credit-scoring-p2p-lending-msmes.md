# The Implementation of Credit Scoring in Order to Analyze the Importance of Non-Performing Loans on Peer-to-Peer Lending towards Credit Distribution for Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises

## Bibliographic Information

- Authors: Hedwigis Esti Riwayati, Aviliani, and Frida Yuniar Prastika
- Year: 2022
- Journal: International Business and Accounting Research Journal
- Volume and issue: 6(2)
- Pages: 137–147
- Article: [View journal PDF](https://journal.stebilampung.ac.id/index.php/ibarj/article/viewFile/248/171)

## Research Focus

This study examines credit scoring in relation to non-performing loans and credit distribution through peer-to-peer lending, with a focus on financing for micro, small and medium enterprises (MSMEs).

## Research Themes

- Credit scoring
- Non-performing loans
- Peer-to-peer lending
- MSME financing
- Credit risk
